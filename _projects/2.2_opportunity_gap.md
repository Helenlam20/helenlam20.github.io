The dashboard illustrates how the conditions in which children grow up correlate with their outcomes from birth to adulthood, specifically regarding health and well-being, education, work and income, and housing.

Individuals are mapped to the location where they grew up (for 35-year-olds, this is their location at age 15) rather than where they currently live. Their outcomes are consistently measured using the most recent data available.

We make our results publicly available as data sets on:
